1.	INTRODUCTION 
Nana Advocacy and Empowerment Foundation (NAEF) is a non-governmental, non-profit, and volunteer-driven organization committed to advancing social justice, gender equality, education, civic participation, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment. 
NAEF operates through structured program pillars designed to address critical development challenges affecting vulnerable populations, especially women, girls, youth, and underserved communities. 
The organization works through grassroots engagement, partnerships, advocacy campaigns, capacity building, and community-based interventions. 
 
2.	VISION, MISSION & CORE VALUES 
Vision   
Empowered, informed, and resilient communities where every individual lives with dignity, equal opportunity, and access to sustainable development. 
Mission   
To promote social justice, gender equality, inclusive governance, education, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment through advocacy, capacity building, and strategic partnerships. 
Core Values: 
1.	Integrity   
2.	Accountability   
3.	Inclusion   
4.	Service   
5.	Collaboration
6.	Impact
7.	Transparency

9.	CODE OF CONDUCT 
 "Advocacy Starts With You" 
1.	PURPOSE 
The Nana Advocacy and Empowerment Foundation (NAEF) is a non-profit, non-governmental, and volunteer-driven organization committed to promoting social justice, gender equality, quality education, inclusive governance, public health awareness, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment. 
This Code of Conduct outlines the standards of behaviour expected from all individuals representing NAEF. It is designed to uphold the Foundation's values, protect the communities we serve, promote professionalism, and ensure accountability across all levels of the organization. 
 
2.	SCOPE 
This Code applies to: 
	Board of Trustees members 
	Advisory Council members 
	Executive Management Team 
	Staff members 
	State Coordinators 
	Program Leads and Project Officers 
	Volunteers and Interns 
	Consultants and Partners acting on behalf of NAEF 

The Code applies during official duties, meetings, trainings, field activities, events, online engagements, and any situation where an individual is representing NAEF. 

3.	CORE VALUES 
All members of NAEF shall uphold the following values: 
	Integrity 
	Service 
	Inclusion 
	Accountability 
	Collaboration 
	Transparency 
	Impact 
 
4.	PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT 
All representatives of NAEF shall: 
	Demonstrate honesty, professionalism, and respect in all interactions. 
	Treat colleagues, beneficiaries, partners, and stakeholders fairly and with dignity. 
	Promote teamwork, collaboration, and mutual respect. 
	Avoid any behaviour that may damage the reputation of NAEF. 
	Perform assigned responsibilities diligently and ethically. 

5.	INCLUSION, EQUALITY, AND RESPECT 
NAEF maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward discrimination, harassment, bullying, victimization, and abuse. 
No individual shall be discriminated against on the basis of: 
	Gender 
	Ethnicity 
	Religion 
	Disability 
	Age 
	Social or economic status 
	Tribe or cultural background 
All persons shall be treated with dignity, fairness, and respect. 

6.	SAFEGUARDING AND BENEFICIARY PROTECTION 
NAEF is committed to protecting children, young people, women, and vulnerable adults from all forms of abuse, exploitation, neglect, and harm. 
All representatives must: 
	Prioritize the safety and wellbeing of beneficiaries. 
	Maintain professional boundaries with beneficiaries. 
	Avoid situations that may place themselves or beneficiaries at risk. 
	Obtain informed consent before collecting personal information, photographs, videos, or testimonials. 
	Obtain parental or guardian consent before engaging with minors. 
Sexual exploitation, abuse, harassment, or inappropriate relationships with beneficiaries are strictly prohibited. 
Any safeguarding concern must be reported immediately through appropriate channels. 

7.	CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION 
All members must protect confidential information obtained through their work with NAEF. 
This includes: 
	Beneficiary records 
	Personal data 
	Internal reports 
	Financial information 
	Donor information 
	Organizational documents 
Confidential information must not be disclosed without authorization unless required by law or safeguarding obligations. 
 
8.	INTEGRITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY 
Representatives shall: 
	Provide accurate information in reports and communications. 
	Maintain truthful records and documentation. 
	Use organizational resources responsibly. 
	Comply with approved policies and procedures. 
	Take responsibility for their actions and decisions. 
Falsification of information, reports, attendance records, financial records, or program data is strictly prohibited. 

9.	USE OF ORGANIZATIONAL RESOURCES 
NAEF resources, including funds, equipment, materials, vehicles, communication platforms, and intellectual property, shall only be used for approved organizational purposes. 
Members shall not misuse organizational resources for personal benefit. 
 
10.	CONFLICT OF INTEREST 
A conflict of interest occurs when personal, family, financial, political, or professional interests interfere with objective decision-making. 
All representatives must: 
	Declare any actual or potential conflict of interest. 
	Refrain from participating in decisions where such conflicts exist. 
	Act in the best interests of NAEF and the communities it serves. 
 
11.	ANTI-CORRUPTION AND BRIBERY 
NAEF prohibits all forms of corruption, bribery, fraud, extortion, kickbacks, and unethical financial practices. 
Representatives shall not: 
	Offer or accept bribes. 
	Request unauthorized payments. 
	Misappropriate organizational funds. 
	Engage in fraudulent activities. 
All financial transactions must be transparent, documented, and approved through established procedures. 

12.	SOCIAL MEDIA AND PUBLIC COMMUNICATION 
Members representing NAEF online or in public spaces shall: 
	Share accurate information. 
	Communicate respectfully. 
	Protect confidential information. 
	Avoid hate speech, harassment, or discriminatory content. 
No individual may issue official statements on behalf of NAEF without authorization. 
 
13.	 USE OF NAEF NAME, LOGO, AND IDENTITY 
The name, logo, branding materials, and official identity of NAEF shall only be used for approved organizational purposes. 
Members shall not: 
	Raise funds in the name of NAEF without authorization. 
	Use NAEF branding for personal projects. 
	Misrepresent themselves as official spokespersons without approval. 
 
14.	REPORTING MISCONDUCT 
All members have a responsibility to report: 
	Safeguarding violations 
	Fraud or corruption 
	Harassment or discrimination 
	Abuse of power 
	Misuse of organizational resources 
	Any violation of this Code 
Reports shall be treated confidentially and investigated fairly. 
Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited. 
 
15.	DISCIPLINARY MEASURES 
Violations of this Code may result in: 
	Verbal warning 
	Written warning 
	Suspension 
	Removal from assigned duties 
	Termination of engagement with NAEF 
	Referral to relevant authorities where necessary 
Disciplinary actions shall be determined based on the severity of the violation. 
 
16.	 COMMITMENT 
All staffs, volunteers, interns, consultants, and partners are expected to read, understand, and comply with this Code of Conduct. 
By serving under NAEF, individuals commit themselves to upholding the Foundation's vision, mission, values, and ethical standards. 
Together, we will build an organization that reflects integrity, accountability, inclusion, and lasting impact. 
Advocacy Starts With You.
